| | |
|---|---|
| Priority | Must have |
| Persona | Consultant |
| Skill | `pricing` |

**User story:** As a consultant, I want to help to understand and change price plans, including new steps and attributes, location and frame contract pricing.

**Acceptance criteria (PRD):** The current price plan is explained step by step. Changes are previewed with their price effect, approved, applied and tested on a sample quote.

## Flow
`preview_esm_price explainPlan=true → apply_esm_change changeType=pricingPlanStep preview + preview_esm_price proposedStep → approve → commit → preview_esm_price (test)`

Every change follows the golden rules in PLAYBOOK.md (preview → explicit approval → commit → verify → summary with log id).

## Test script (done when every line passes)
1. [ ] Ask: "Explain our price plan" → ordered steps.
2. [ ] Ask: "Add a regional surcharge step" → Approval with before/after steps and sample price effect.
3. [ ] Approve → applied; sample re-priced shows the step; undo offered.
